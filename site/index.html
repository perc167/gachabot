Set it up in a few minutes

Pick what you want to use. Each part has a few short steps, in order.

Six steps. The first two matter most, the rest is taste.

01

Add the bot

Run $invite in a server that already has it, or use the button at the top of this page. It asks for what every part needs: messages and embeds for the gacha, roles, kicks, bans and timeouts for moderation, and moving members for voice. Untick anything for a part you won't use.

02

Give it one channel

Left alone the bot answers everywhere, which gets old fast in a busy server.

$channelallow #bot-chat

$channellist shows which channels are allowed, and $channelclear lets it answer everywhere again.

03

Stop commands in other channels

The step above makes the bot ignore commands elsewhere; the message still sits there. A Discord AutoMod rule deletes it.

Server Settings → AutoMod → Create your own → Custom Keywords → Block Message, and exempt your bot channel. The command list to paste is generated by the bot's own tooling, so it never goes stale.

04

Pick a pace

One command sets five dials at once, so nobody has to learn the settings table on day one.

$preset relaxed  ·  $preset normal  ·  $preset slow

$settings shows every dial afterwards, and $set <key> <value> changes one.

05

Decide what can roll

The roster carries games and Western cartoons as well as anime. A server that only wants anime can say so.

$togglegames  ·  $togglewestern

$toggles lists every switch and where it stands. They are all reversible.

06

Point people at the tutorial

$tuto walks a new player through the bot in 17 steps and pays them for finishing each one. They learn the commands by using them rather than by reading a list.

Five steps. After the third, every command works.

01

Put the bot above your staff

Server Settings → Roles: drag the bot's role above your staff roles. It can only jail, mute or ban people whose top role is below its own.

02

Give your staff their permissions

The bot follows Discord's own permissions, so staff ranks are just roles.

Trial Mods need Timeout Members: jail, timeouts, mutes and warnings.
Mods also need Kick Members and Ban Members.

03

Run setup

Name your staff roles and the bot makes everything else: Jailed, Image Muted and Reaction Muted roles, a #jail channel and a #mod-logs channel only staff can see.

$setupmod @Mod @Trial Mod
04

Choose what happens automatically

Optional. Punish people when they reach a number of warnings, and word the bot's messages your own way.

$warnpunish add 3 timeout 1h $invoke set jail message {user.mention} is in jail: {reason}
05

Track who's ready for promotion

Every jail and mute gives the staff member who did it a point. Mods give points by hand for reports that end in a ban.

$point @trialmod ban $toppoints

$modhistory @trialmod counts every action someone has taken.

Three steps. The first one is all you need.

01

Make Join To Create

Makes a category with a Join To Create voice channel and a #vc-info channel with the commands in it. To use a voice channel you already have, give its id.

$vc setup $vc setup 123456789012345678

Only let one bot run Join To Create, or both will make a room every time someone joins.

02

Let people run their rooms

Whoever joins gets their own channel, deleted when it empties. They lock it, hide it, rename it and choose who gets in, from the channel's own chat.

$vc lock · $vc permit @friend · $vc rename chill

$vc panel posts the full command list anywhere.

03

Reward time in voice

Every minute in voice is counted, except in the AFK channel. Hand out roles at milestones.

$vc hours add 10 @Regular $vc lb

It works out of the box. The rest is up to you.

01

Nothing to switch on

Levels start counting the moment the bot is in your server. Chatting earns XP once a minute, and voice earns it too when there's someone to talk to.

$level · $leaderboard
02

Reward the milestones

Hand out roles at levels. Anyone already past a level gets the role with one command.

$levels role add 10 @Active $levels sync
03

Change it on the web

Open the dashboard, log in with Discord and pick your server: XP per message and per minute in voice, level roles, the level-up message and where it goes, and channels that earn nothing. $dashboard in Discord DMs you a private link to the same page.

Every setting is a command too: $levels settings shows them all.

04

Tidy it up

Choose where level-ups are announced, and keep spam or bot channels from earning XP.

$levels channel #level-ups $levels ignore #bot-spam

Every 5 levels pays a little karuda, 100 by default, and only once per milestone, so it never outpaces $daily. $levels karuda 0 turns it off.

Two quick ones, and the rest just works.

01

Roles for new members

Pick the roles everyone gets when they join from a menu: no typing role names. Bots never get them.

$autorole
02

Welcome and goodbye

One command turns on a friendly welcome with their picture. Change the words whenever you like, with fill-ins for their name, the server and the member count.

$welcome #welcome $welcome message Hey {user}, welcome to {server}! $goodbye #welcome

Both are on the dashboard too, under Welcome & roles.

03

The everyday extras

Nothing to set up: snipe deleted and edited messages, go AFK, set reminders, look people and the server up, and copy emojis in.

$snipe · $afk · $remind 10m stretch $userinfo · $serverinfo · $steal
